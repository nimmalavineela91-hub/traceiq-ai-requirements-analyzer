# Digital Payments and Order Processing Platform

## Business Overview
The organization is modernizing its digital checkout and payment processing platform to improve payment success rates, reduce customer drop-off, strengthen fraud controls, and provide better visibility into failed transactions.

## Business Objectives
- Improve payment transaction reliability
- Provide customers with clear payment status notifications
- Reduce duplicate payment attempts
- Improve traceability between requirements and testing
- Detect coverage gaps before production releases
- Support faster impact analysis when requirements change

## Key Stakeholders
- Product Owner
- Business Analyst
- Payments SME
- QA Lead
- Engineering Lead
- Customer Support
- Risk and Compliance

## Core Business Capabilities
- Customer checkout
- Payment authorization
- Payment failure handling
- Retry processing
- Customer notifications
- Refunds
- Fraud validation
- Transaction reporting

## Key Business Problem
Requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and test cases are maintained across multiple documents and tools. When a requirement changes, teams often spend significant time identifying affected user stories, tests, business rules, and application modules.

The objective of TraceIQ AI is to automatically analyze these artifacts, create traceability relationships, detect missing coverage, and identify downstream change impact.
